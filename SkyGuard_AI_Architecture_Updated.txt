========================================================

 1. OFFICIAL PROBLEM

========================================================

Problem Statement ID:

SIH26073

Title:

AI/ML-Based Intelligent Anomaly Detection for Automatic Weather Stations (AWS)

Organization:

Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES)

India Meteorological Department (IMD)

Theme:

Disaster Management

The system must detect abnormal, inconsistent, corrupted, or faulty observations from Automatic Weather Stations in real time.

STRICT RAW INPUT CONSTRAINT:

The system must use ONLY these three raw meteorological sensor parameters:

 1. Temperature (°C)

 2. Atmospheric Pressure (hPa)

 3. Relative Humidity (%)

Do not require wind, rainfall, radiation, soil moisture, visibility, cloud cover, radar, satellite, or any other sensor as an input to the core anomaly detector.

Additional information such as timestamp, station ID, latitude, longitude, elevation, and neighboring station identity may be treated as metadata/context only.

Derived physical variables calculated mathematically from T/P/RH are allowed.
